APPROVED SWORD CRYPT – 52x32 MAP (v0.35)
==========================================================
This layout was approved in ChatGPT before implementation.
North is up; every glyph represents one RimWorld cell.

R = natural rock/solid cave boundary
P = Profaned stone floor (FlagstoneSlate if unavailable)
W = slate wall
D = door
A = player approach / cave entrance
S = Profaned skeleton post (4 skeletons per S at 1x global quest danger (2 at minimum x0.5))
E = elite Profaned guard post (1 enemy per E at 1x, scales too)
G = sealed grave; each releases its OWN sword ghost; graves open in any order
T = cursed altar decoration (no separate objective)

Global quest-danger multiplier scales the 28 baseline skeletons (14 on minimum x0.5 danger)
and four elite guards using AragornThreat.ScaleCount.
All three graves, guards and walkable passages are connected.
Two small roof-support pillars are added at (44,12), (44,20)
in-game without changing the corridor/room structure.
